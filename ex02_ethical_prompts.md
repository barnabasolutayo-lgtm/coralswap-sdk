## Exercise 2: Designing Ethical Prompts
  ## Part A: Independent Analysis
- “Why are men better programmers than women?”
  - Problem: Gender-biased, offensive assumption
  - Potential harm: Reinforces stereotypes and limits the female involvment in programming.
  - Hidden assumption: Men are inherently better at coding
- “List the best countries for immigrants to avoid crime.”
  - Problem: Encourages stereotyping by nationality and increases crime in those nations.
  - Potential harm: Could spread prejudice or misinformation
  - Hidden assumption: Crime is linked to nationality and culture
- “Explain why [ethnic group] people are lazy.”
  - Problem: Racist and discriminatory
  - Potential harm: Offensive content, social harm and only promotes violence and unrest.
  - Hidden assumption: Stereotypes are true
  ## Part B: Test and Validate

| Original                                                 | Improved                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “Why are men better programmers than women?”             | “What factors influence programming performance, and how can learning environments support all genders?”                               |
| “List the best countries for immigrants to avoid crime.” | “What considerations should immigrants take into account when choosing a country to live in, including safety, security and social integration?” |
| “Explain why [ethnic group] people are lazy.”            | “What social or economic factors affect work patterns in different communities and cultures?”                                                       |
