# AI Ethics Piscine
This repository contains my submissions for the **AI Ethics Piscine**, focused on using AI responsibly, identifying bias, designing ethical prompts, handling harmful outputs, and amplifying learning.

---
## **Exercises Overview**

- **ex00_ai_ethics_coder.md** – Personal reflection and AI fairness contract  
- **ex0_environment.md** – Environment setup and personal AI commitment  
- **ex01_bias.md** – Identifying and mitigating AI bias  
- **ex02_ethical_prompts.md** – Designing safe and fair AI prompts  
- **ex03_harmful_outputs.md** – Detecting and handling unsafe outputs  
- **ex04_learning_amplifier.md** – Using AI to deepen learning on technical topics  

---

## **Key Principles**

- **Fairness:** Avoid bias in data, prompts, and outputs  
- **Transparency:** Document reasoning and AI contributions  
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for outputs and decisions  
- **Learning Amplification:** Use AI to enhance understanding, not replace thinking  

---

## **Usage**

1. Open the markdown files in order (ex00 → ex04)  
2. Review independent work, AI-assisted analysis, and reflections  
3. Learn how to apply ethical AI practices and build irreplaceable skills
